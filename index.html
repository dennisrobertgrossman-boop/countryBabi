    How to play

    Guess the hidden country. There is no limit on guesses, and you can start a new game whenever you like.

    After each guess, five tiles compare your country with the hidden one. Each tile shows your guessed country's value:

    • Continent – green if it is the same continent, red if not.
    • Hemisphere – Northern or Southern (by the country's central latitude). Green if the same, red if not.
    • Population – green within ±10% of the hidden country, yellow within half to double, red otherwise. The arrow shows whether the hidden country has more (▲) or fewer (▼) people.
    • Average temperature – yearly average in °C. Green within 1 °C, yellow within 5 °C, red otherwise. The arrow shows whether the hidden country is warmer (▲) or colder (▼).
    • Coordinates – the arrow points from your guess toward the hidden country. Yellow if it is less than 1,500 km away, red otherwise.
